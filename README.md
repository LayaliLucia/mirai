# 🌟 Mirai – Your Gateway to the Future

**Mirai** is a business idea developed as part of our **Entrepreneurship project**.  
The concept was designed to address a specific market opportunity and presented as a pitch, including key elements of a business plan.

## ✨ Project Overview
In this project, we:
- Designed and refined the **business idea: Mirai**
- Created a **Business Requirements Document (BRD)**
- Developed a **pitch presentation** to communicate the value proposition
- Presented the project to our professor as part of the final assessment

## 🎯 Purpose
The goal of Mirai is to:
- Provide innovative solutions that act as a **gateway to the future**
- Demonstrate creativity and entrepreneurship skills
- Apply business planning frameworks in practice
- Communicate ideas effectively through documentation and presentation

## 📌 Notes
This project was an academic exercise in entrepreneurship.  
It reflects our ability to move from **idea → prototype → pitch**, combining creativity, planning, and communication.
